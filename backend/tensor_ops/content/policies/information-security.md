# Information Security Policy — {{company_name}}

**Effective:** {{effective_date}} · **Owner:** {{security_owner}} · **Approved by:** {{approver}} · **Review:** every 12 months

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out how {{company_name}} protects the confidentiality, integrity and availability of its information
and the information customers trust it with.

## 2. Scope
All employees, contractors and systems that store, process or transmit {{company_name}} or customer information,
including our production environment on {{cloud_provider}}.

## 3. Responsibilities
- **{{approver}}** approves this policy set and reviews security at least once a year.
- **{{security_owner}}** owns the security programme, maintains the policies and reports on risks and incidents.
- **Every staff member** follows these policies, completes security training and reports suspected incidents immediately.

## 4. Policy statements
1. Information is classified and handled according to the Data Classification and Retention Policy.
2. Access is granted on least privilege and removed promptly (Access Control Policy).
3. Production changes are reviewed and approved before release (Change Management Policy).
4. Security events are reported and handled under the Incident Response Plan.
5. Vendors with access to our data are assessed before and during use (Vendor Management Policy).
6. Risks are assessed at least yearly and treated by named owners.
7. Exceptions to any policy are documented, time-limited and approved by {{security_owner}}.

## 5. Compliance
Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action. Policy compliance is reviewed during the yearly management review.
