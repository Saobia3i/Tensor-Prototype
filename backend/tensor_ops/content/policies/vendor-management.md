# Vendor Management Policy — {{company_name}}

**Effective:** {{effective_date}} · **Owner:** {{security_owner}} · **Review:** every 12 months

1. All vendors that store or process company or customer data are recorded in the vendor inventory with an owner and criticality.
2. Before onboarding a critical vendor we review its security assurance (for example a SOC 2 report or ISO certificate) and sign a data processing agreement where personal data is involved.
3. Critical vendors are reviewed every 12 months; issues found are tracked to resolution or the risk is accepted by {{approver}}.
4. Vendor access is removed when the relationship ends.
