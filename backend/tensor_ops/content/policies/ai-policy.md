# AI Policy — {{company_name}}

**Effective:** {{effective_date}} · **AI owner:** {{ai_owner}} · **Review:** every 12 months

1. **Purpose.** {{company_name}} develops and uses AI to benefit customers while managing risks to people and the business.
2. **Accountability.** {{ai_owner}} is accountable for AI risk; product teams own their AI features.
3. **Impact assessment.** New or materially changed AI features are assessed for impact on individuals and groups before release.
4. **Data.** Data used to build or run AI has documented provenance, permissions and quality checks; customer data is used only as contracts allow.
5. **Validation and monitoring.** Models are evaluated against defined criteria before release and monitored in production.
6. **Human oversight.** Decisions with significant effects on people keep a human able to review and override.
7. **Transparency.** Customers are told when they interact with AI features and how to raise concerns.
