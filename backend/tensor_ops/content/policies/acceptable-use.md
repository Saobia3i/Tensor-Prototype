# Acceptable Use and Confidentiality Policy — {{company_name}}

**Effective:** {{effective_date}} · **Owner:** {{security_owner}}

By signing, staff agree to:
1. Keep company and customer information confidential during and after employment.
2. Use company devices that are managed, encrypted and kept up to date; report lost devices immediately.
3. Use only approved tools for company data; do not paste customer data into unapproved AI or file-sharing services.
4. Complete security awareness training at onboarding and every year.
5. Report suspected phishing, incidents or policy breaches to {{security_owner}}.
